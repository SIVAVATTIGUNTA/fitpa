About Us

Empowering India Together

FITPA is an international non-profit federation that connects technology enterprises with the global expertise of Indian-origin entrepreneurs. With strong roots in the U.S. and U.K., and a growing presence across Europe and the Middle East, FITPA fosters business growth through strategic networking, global partnerships, and access to emerging markets. It serves as a vital bridge between global tech ecosystems and the innovation and leadership of the Indian diaspora.

Challenges Faced by Indian-Origin Entrepreneurs

Indian-origin entrepreneurs in the United Kingdom and the United States confront a distinct set of challenges that encompass navigating cultural differences, securing finance, and cultivating robust professional networks—challenges that are in addition to the common obstacles encountered by all entrepreneurs. These individuals may also encounter nuanced forms of prejudice and systemic barriers within their respective business ecosystems.

Obtaining sufficient funding often proves to be a considerable challenge, particularly for those lacking established local networks or familiarity with financial institutions.

Successfully adapting to new business environments requires an understanding of local market dynamics and the ability to navigate cultural subtleties in both communication and business practices.

Establishing a strong network of contacts, mentors, and potential investors is vital for entrepreneurial success but may pose difficulties, especially for newcomers.

FITPA's Role

Empowering Success & Overcoming Barriers

As a dynamic federation, FITPA occupies a unique and pivotal position in strengthening the ecosystem for Indian-origin entrepreneurs worldwide by offering multifaceted support:


Connecting to Funding & Investors

Crucial bridge to venture capitalists, angel investors, private equity funds, and institutional lenders; specialized forums and pitch events.

Upskilling & Knowledge Transfer

Collaborates with industry leaders and academic institutions for targeted skill development programs, workshops, and mentorship.

From Startups to Acquisitions

Guidance on structuring partnerships, negotiating contracts, and accessing supply chains; brokers strategic alliances and M&A assistance..

Board Member Recruitment & Corporate Governance

Supports identifying and recruiting experienced board members through a curated database, enhancing credibility and appeal to investors.

Enabling Public Listings: From the UK Stock Exchange to NASDAQ

Guidance for regulatory compliance, corporate restructuring, and securing advisory teams for IPOs on prestigious platforms

Lobbying & Policy Advocacy: Bridging India, the UK, the USA

Engages with government bodies to champion regulatory reforms, economic incentives, and bilateral agreements.
