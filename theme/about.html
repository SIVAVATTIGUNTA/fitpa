Who We Are

FITPA is an international, not-for-profit federation committed to fostering connections between technology enterprises and the global expertise of entrepreneurs of Indian origin including those from American, British, and German Indian communities. With a strong foundation in the United Kingdom and United States, and a rapidly growing presence throughout Europe, the Middle East, and beyond, FITPA enables businesses to expand and thrive through strategic networking and access to emerging markets.

Whether your objective is to enter new markets or build dynamic, cross-border teams, FITPA acts as a crucial link—integrating the worldʼs technology ecosystems with the innovation, leadership, and entrepreneurial excellence of the Indian diaspora.

FITPA serves as a launchpad for tech-driven ventures, helping them find support, opportunity, and growth through our extensive network of global partners.
